# E007 pilot (exploratory; seeds 201-216, disjoint from every registered seed)

S5 generated by its four adjacent transpositions s1..s4. The ordered pairs (s1, s2) and (s3, s4) never
appear next to each other in training. Training uses all words of length 1-5 without them (790 words,
65 of 120 labels). The readout is fitted on long words that also avoid the pairs (CLEAN), and scored
on fresh CLEAN words and on long words that contain the held-out pairs (HELD). Raw lines are in the
`.jsonl` files beside this one.

- **HELDOUT arm** (per-letter unitary operators, as in E005), 16 seeds: 4 succeeded on CLEAN words at
  L = 160 (seeds 202, 211, 212, 214), and all 4 scored 1.0000 on HELD words too. The 12 that failed
  were near chance on both.
- **PAIR arm** (the memorisation alternative made concrete: one operator per ordered pair of letters,
  so a held-out pair's operator is never trained), 12 seeds: HELD accuracy at L = 160 was at most
  0.022 on every seed. On seed 216 it learned CLEAN words perfectly (1.0000 at L = 160) and still
  scored 0.015 on HELD words: the signature of a model that only knows the transitions it saw.
- **FULL arm** (no pairs held out), 4 seeds: 1 success (seed 201). Too few to compare success rates.
- The relator score is less reliable with four generators than in E006: seed 201 HELDOUT scored 5 and
  failed; seed 209 scored 6 and failed. E006's threshold does not transfer to this setting without
  its own calibration, and E007 does not use it.

Success is rarer here (4 of 16) than in E005, so the registered run uses 40 seeds.
