# E010: does held-out composition survive a change of group? Registration

Status: **Registered** (2026-09-27), after the pilot on seeds 101-110
(`results/exploratory/E010_pilot.md`) and before any run on the registered seeds 601-640.

## What is being attacked

E007 showed, on S5 with adjacent transpositions, that per-letter operators trained without two
transitions still handle them. That could be a property of that one presentation. E010 repeats it on
a different group and presentation: A5 (60 states), generated by the 3-cycles
g0 = (0 1 2), g1 = (1 2 3), g2 = (2 3 4). The ordered pairs (g0, g1) and (g1, g2) never occur in training.

## Frozen setup

- Training: all words of length 1-6 over {g0, g1, g2} that avoid both pairs (349 words), labelled by
  their permutation. 8 × 8 unitary operators, `veritas_holo/learn.py`, as E007.
- HELDOUT arm: one operator per letter. PAIR arm (the null): one operator per ordered pair plus one per
  first letter, as E007.
- Readout: nearest centroid on CLEAN long words; scored on fresh CLEAN words and on HELD words
  (words that contain the pairs), L = 40 and 160. Success: CLEAN accuracy ≥ 0.99 at L = 160.
- Seeds 601-640, both arms. Runner: `experiments/E010_a5_composition/run.py` (defaults: seeds
  601-640, maximum training length 6).

## Registered predictions

- **H1 (composition)** At least 30 HELDOUT runs succeed, and every one of them scores ≥ 0.99 on HELD
  words at L = 160.
- **H2 (the null fails where it must)** Every PAIR run scores < 0.05 on HELD words at L = 160.
- **H3 (the null is not simply weak)** At least 30 PAIR runs succeed on CLEAN words.

## Limits

- As in E007, composition is built into the per-letter architecture. The question is whether training
  without those transitions still fixes the operators correctly for them.
- Two groups tested (S5 in E007, A5 here), one width, one choice of held-out pairs each.
