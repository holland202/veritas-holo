## What this shows

- **Every learned model that got the training transitions right also got the held-out ones right.**
  12 of 40 HELDOUT runs learned the task on words without the held-out pairs. All 12 scored 1.0000 at
  L = 160 on words full of transitions (s1 then s2, s3 then s4) that never appeared in any training
  word.
- **The memorisation alternative fails exactly where it should.** 13 PAIR runs also learned the words
  they could see, perfectly. On words with the held-out transitions, every PAIR run stayed at chance
  (at most 0.0265). Both arms learned what they were shown; only the per-letter operators carried
  over to what they were not shown.
- **What this does and does not establish.** Composition is built into a product of per-letter
  matrices; E007 shows that training **without** those transitions still pins down the operators
  correctly for them. It does not show that the architecture invented composition, and training
  still fails on most seeds (12 of 40 succeeded).
