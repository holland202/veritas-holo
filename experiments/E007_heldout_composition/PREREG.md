# E007: do learned operators compose transitions they never saw? Registration

Status: **Registered** (2026-09-27), after the pilot on seeds 201-216
(`results/exploratory/E007_pilot.md`) and before any run on the registered seeds 301-340.

## The alternative explanation being attacked

E005's success could be read as "the model learned the transitions it was shown, well enough to
interpolate". E007 removes two transitions from training entirely. S5 is generated by its four
adjacent transpositions s1..s4, and the ordered pairs (s1, s2) and (s3, s4) never occur next to each
other in any training word. The test words contain them many times. A model that learned per-letter
operators should handle them; a model that memorised transitions cannot.

## Frozen setup

- Training: all words of length 1-5 over {s1..s4} that avoid both pairs (790 words), labelled by
  their permutation. Training as E005 (`veritas_holo/learn.py`; 8 × 8 unitary; 3000 steps).
- **HELDOUT arm:** one operator per letter.
- **PAIR arm (the null):** one operator per ordered pair of letters, plus one per first letter. It sees
  the same words and labels, and the held-out pairs' operators never receive a gradient. This is the
  memorisation alternative made concrete.
- Readout: nearest centroid fitted on 6000 long words that **avoid** the pairs (CLEAN). Scored on 2000
  fresh CLEAN words and on 2000 random words that **contain** the pairs (HELD). L = 40 and 160.
  Success means CLEAN accuracy ≥ 0.99 at L = 160.
- Seeds 301-340, both arms. Runner: `experiments/E007_heldout_composition/run.py`.

## Registered predictions

- **H1 (composition)** At least 5 HELDOUT runs succeed, and every one of them also scores ≥ 0.99 on
  HELD words at L = 160.
- **H2 (the null fails where it must)** Every PAIR run scores < 0.05 on HELD words at L = 160.
- **H3 (the null is not simply weak)** At least 1 PAIR run succeeds on CLEAN words. The contrast is then
  between models that both learned what they saw, and differ on what they did not see.

## Limits

- Composition is built into the per-letter architecture: a product of learned matrices applies to any
  order of letters. The question E007 answers is whether training **without** those transitions still
  fixes the operators correctly for them. It does not claim the architecture discovers composition
  itself.
- Success is rare in this setting (4 of 16 in the pilot). E007 conditions on success and does not
  claim reliability; E006's restart check is not recalibrated here.
- One group, one width, one pair of held-out transitions.
